# Do I Look Like a _?

**Date:** 2024-02-22

## Concept

> **“Do I look like a _?”**
>
> ✔️ → “You don’t EVEN know me😡!”
>
> ✖️ → “You don’t know me👻.”

Judgements were, are, and will be made in milliseconds.

Loudly or silently.
Intentionally or unconsciously.

Does the truth matter?

The work explores how quickly people categorize others based on appearance, identity, and pre-existing ideas.

The blank in the title can be filled with almost anything.

## Interaction

Participants are presented with a series of words describing different identities, categories, or assumptions.

They can respond using two buttons:

* **YES** → “You don’t EVEN know me😡!”
* **NO** → “You don’t know me👻.”

A third button moves to the next word.

The words are deliberately mixed across different kinds of categories, including:

> terrorist · virus · boy · girl · she · he · they · racist · ghost · inferior · outsider · pest · nobody · trash · drunk · addict · criminal · murderer · slut · misogyny · coward · robot · alien · cyborg

The interaction asks the participant to make a judgement almost immediately, while simultaneously exposing the instability of that judgement.

## Technical Experiment

The project uses physical buttons as a simple interface for **categorization and response**.

The system presents one word at a time and records the participant's choice through direct interaction.

The OLED display provides the changing prompt and response, while the buttons create a simple decision structure:

**Word → Judgement → Response → Next**

The deliberately simple interface mirrors the speed and simplicity with which categorization can happen in everyday life.

## Disclaimer

> *What the participants choose does not stand for what they really think. It is just for demonstration.*
